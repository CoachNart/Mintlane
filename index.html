Questions, answered.

The essentials before you launch or explore.

What is Mintlane?

Mintlane is a token launchpad on Solana. Creators can define a token, review its launch details and approve the on-chain transaction, and anyone can explore projects as they become available.

Which wallets can I use?

Connect a supported Solana wallet, such as Phantom, from the Connect Wallet button in the top right.

Do I need a wallet?

You can browse without one. To launch a token you'll need to connect a supported Solana wallet and approve the transaction yourself.

What does it cost to launch?

Launching an on-chain token requires a small network fee paid in SOL from your wallet. You'll see the details in your wallet before you approve anything.

Are the projects shown on Mintlane verified?

Projects shown here are not endorsed by Mintlane. Always check a token's contract address and do your own research. Where verification is supported, it's shown on the token's card.

Is anything on this page financial advice?

No. Mintlane provides tools to create and explore tokens. Nothing on the platform is investment, legal or tax advice.
